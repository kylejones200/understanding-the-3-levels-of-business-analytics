It is a set of technologies capable of extracting what is really
relevant for the company, and it should act as a compass so that the
company can measure risks and opportunities and predict results.
Business Analytics is based on descriptive, predictive or prescriptive
data analysis. It is used for existing data to provide the knowledge
necessary to solve current problems and opportunities, predict future
problems and opportunities, and how to deal with current as well as
future problems and opportunities that arise from an action plan. It
also allows us to discover current opportunities and future trends for
customers regarding your product / service. This technology methodically
analyzes an organization's data in a repetitive or iterative way to gain
new insights and understanding of business performance, emphasizing
statistical analysis. The study of this data allows companies to answer
questions such as \"What is happening?\", \"Why is it happening?\" And
what is going to happen?\". We can explain Business Analytics as the set
of tools that help in decision-making at all levels of the company.
Helping companies better understand, anticipate, and shape business
results through the ability to identify and analyze trends, patterns,
and anomalies; compare what-if cases and predict potential threats and
opportunities, as well as plan, budget, and forecast resources.

Business decision making is faster, more effective and more forceful;

technologies to analyze historical data to gain new knowledge and
improve strategic decision making. It combines the fields of management,

use of methodologies such as data mining, predictive analysis, and
statistical analysis to analyze and transform data into useful
information, identify and anticipate trends and results, and ultimately
make more business decisions. smart and data-driven.

There are three main methods of business analysis:

- Descriptive: Interpreting historical data to identify trends and
  patterns
- Predictive: Using statistics to forecast future results
- Prescriptive: The application of tests and other techniques to
  determine which result will be the best in a given scenario
